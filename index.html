How an alert works求助怎么运作

From one tap to a patroller at your side从点一下，到救援队员赶到身边

Tap a step to see the screen. The skier and the ski patrol see the same alert, updated in real time.点击每一步看对应的画面。求助的人和救援队看到的是同一条实时更新的求助。

Skier's phone · AlpMate滑雪者的手机 · 雪伴
Location you can trust靠得住的定位

Your real position, not a guess用真实的位置，而不是猜测

Rescue starts from your phone's raw GPS and its accuracy circle. Runs on the map are only a reference, so patrol always knows how sure the location is.救援以手机的原始 GPS 和误差范围为准，地图上的雪道只作参考。救援队始终清楚这个位置有多准。

Built for the bad moments为最糟的时刻而设计

Safety nets at every step每一步都有兜底

2-minute escalation2 分钟没人接就升级

If no patroller accepts within two minutes, AlpMate tells you, even on the lock screen, to call the patrol line or 111, with one-tap buttons.两分钟内没有队员接收，雪伴会提醒你（锁屏也能看到）直接拨打雪场救援电话或 111，一键拨号。

111 is always one tap away111 永远一键可拨

AlpMate works alongside emergency services, never instead of them. The resort's patrol line is right there too.雪伴和紧急服务一起用，而不是取代它；雪场的救援电话也在旁边。

Help someone else帮别人求助

Found someone hurt? Report whether they are awake and breathing, how many people, and add a photo. Critical reports jump the queue.遇到有人受伤？报告对方是否清醒、有没有呼吸、几个人，还可以附照片；危急的报告会排在最前。

Medical card, your choice医疗信息卡，你说了算

Blood type, allergies and medications stay on your phone, and go to patrol only with your alert, and only if you turned sharing on.血型、过敏、用药只存在你的手机上；只有你发出求助、并且打开了分享，才会发给救援队。

Talk without a call不打电话也能沟通

Text, voice and one-tap replies like "I can't move", in English and Chinese, for when talking is hard or the wind is loud.文字、语音，和「我动不了」这类一键快捷回复（中英文都有），说话不便或风太大时也能沟通。

Be seen让救援看到你

A rescue light turns your screen and flashlight into a beacon when it's getting dark or visibility is poor.天色暗或能见度低时，求救灯光会让屏幕和闪光灯一起闪烁，方便救援队找到你。

For ski resorts给雪场

AlpMate Patrol雪伴救援

A companion app for your ski patrol. It works alongside your radios and patrol line, and turns a skier's tap into a located, actionable alert.给雪场救援队用的配套 App，和你们现有的对讲机、救援电话一起用，把滑雪者的求助变成带位置、能马上行动的警报。

  • ✓Alerts that ring on the lock screen until a patroller accepts.锁屏也会响，直到有队员点「我去处理」。
  • ✓Nearest on-duty patroller first, with the team's live positions.最近的在岗队员优先，队员位置实时可见。
  • ✓Critical and bystander reports highlighted, with duplicate reports merged.危急报告和旁观者报告突出显示，同一起事故的重复报告自动合并。
  • ✓Incident reports for your safety records and WorkSafe reporting.事故记录单，方便安全统计和向 WorkSafe 报告。

AlpMate is a safety support tool, not an emergency service.雪伴是安全辅助工具，不是紧急服务。

In a life-threatening emergency, call 111 first. Alerting ski patrol only works at resorts using AlpMate Patrol, inside the resort area and while patrol is on duty, and delivery depends on signal, battery and your phone's settings. Rescue is carried out by the resort's own patrol.遇到危及生命的情况，请先拨打 111。通知救援队只在接入雪伴救援的雪场、雪场范围内、救援队值守时间有效，能否送到取决于信号、电量和手机设置。救援由雪场自己的救援队负责。

Privacy隐私

Your data, kept small只收必要的信息

On your phone留在手机上

Profile, emergency contacts and medical card stay on your device.个人资料、紧急联系人和医疗卡只存在你的手机上。

Short-lived自动删除
