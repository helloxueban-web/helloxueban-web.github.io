For skiers and snowboarders给滑雪的人

Everything for a day on the mountain一天滑雪需要的，都在这里

Made for the way New Zealand skis: big mountains, patchy signal and friends who never ski at the same speed.为新西兰的滑雪场景设计：山大、信号时有时无，朋友的速度永远不一样。

Ski tracks雪迹记录

Runs, vertical, distance and top speed for every day, with the barometer for better altitude. Heart rate from Apple Watch.每天的趟数、落差、里程和最高时速；用气压计让高度更准，戴 Apple Watch 还能记录心率。

Your crew on one map小队互相看到

Create a team with a code, see each other on the piste map and chat with text, voice and photos. Locations and messages delete after 24 hours.用队码建小队，在雪道地图上看到彼此，发文字、语音和照片。位置和消息 24 小时后自动删除。

One-tap SOS一键求助

Big, readable coordinates with accuracy, the nearest patrol or medical point, one tap to call 111 or the resort's patrol line, and a text to your emergency contacts.大字显示坐标和误差范围、最近的救援点；一键拨打 111 或雪场救援电话，给紧急联系人发短信。

Alert ski patrol通知雪场救援队

At resorts using AlpMate Patrol, send your location straight to patrollers, see when someone accepts and how far away they are, and message them.在接入雪伴救援的雪场，把位置直接发给救援队员；能看到有没有人接收、队员离你多远，还能互发消息。

Medical card医疗信息卡

Blood type, allergies and medications stay on your phone, and are shared with patrol only when you alert them and choose to.血型、过敏、用药只存在你的手机上；只有你通知救援队、并且选择分享时才会发出。

Help someone else帮别人求助

Found someone hurt? Report what you see — awake, breathing, how many people — with an optional photo, so patrol arrives prepared.遇到有人受伤？报告你看到的情况（清醒吗、有没有呼吸、几个人），可以附一张照片，让救援队有准备地赶来。

For ski resorts给雪场

AlpMate Patrol雪伴救援

A companion app for your ski patrol. It works alongside your radios and patrol line, and turns a skier's tap into a located, actionable alert.给雪场救援队用的配套 App，和你们现有的对讲机、救援电话一起用，把滑雪者的求助变成带位置、能马上行动的警报。

  • ✓Alerts that ring on the lock screen until a patroller accepts.锁屏也会响，直到有队员点「我去处理」。
  • ✓Raw GPS with its accuracy, updated every 15 seconds, with the nearest run as a reference.伤者原始 GPS 和误差范围，每 15 秒更新，附近雪道只作参考。
  • ✓Nearest on-duty patroller first, with live positions of the team.最近的在岗队员优先，队员位置实时可见。
  • ✓Medical card, triage and photos when the skier or a bystander shares them, plus chat with quick replies.求助人或旁观者分享的医疗卡、伤情和照片，还能和对方聊天、快捷回复。
  • ✓Incident reports and duplicate-report merging for your safety records.事故记录单，同一起事故的重复报告自动合并，方便安全统计。

Rescue is carried out by the resort's own patrol.遇到危及生命的情况，请先拨打 111。通知救援队只在接入雪伴救援的雪场、雪场范围内、救援队值守时间有效，能否送到取决于信号、电量和手机设置。救援由雪场自己的救援队负责。

Privacy隐私

Your data, kept small只收必要的信息

On your phone留在手机上

Profile, emergency contacts and medical card stay on your device.个人资料、紧急联系人和医疗卡只存在你的手机上。

Short-lived自动删除
