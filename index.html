Location you can trust靠得住的定位

Rescue starts from your phone's raw GPS and its accuracy circle. Runs on the map are only a reference, so patrol always knows how sure the location is.救援以手机的原始 GPS 和误差范围为准，地图上的雪道只作参考。救援队始终清楚这个位置有多准。

Built for the bad moments为最糟的时刻而设计

Safety nets at every step每一步都有兜底

2-minute escalation2 分钟没人接就升级

If no patroller accepts within two minutes, AlpMate tells you to call the patrol line or 111, with one-tap buttons — on the lock screen too, if notifications are on.两分钟内没有队员接收，雪伴会提醒你直接拨打雪场救援电话或 111，一键拨号；打开了通知的话，锁屏上也能看到。

111 is always one tap away111 永远一键可拨

AlpMate works alongside emergency services, never instead of them. The resort's patrol line is right there too.雪伴和紧急服务一起用，而不是取代它；雪场的救援电话也在旁边。

Help someone else帮别人求助

Found someone hurt? Report whether they are awake and breathing, how many people, and add a photo. Critical reports jump the queue.遇到有人受伤？报告对方是否清醒、有没有呼吸、几个人，还可以附照片；危急的报告会排在最前。

Weak or no signal信号差或没信号

The SOS screen tells you when you're offline, puts calling and texting first, and resends your alert once you're back in range.求助页会告诉你现在没网，打电话、发短信排在最前；回到有信号的地方，求助会自动重发。

Talk without a call不打电话也能沟通

Text, voice and one-tap replies like "I can't move", in English and Chinese, for when talking is hard or the wind is loud.文字、语音，和「我动不了」这类一键快捷回复（中英文都有），说话不便或风太大时也能沟通。

Be seen让救援看到你

A rescue light turns your screen and flashlight into a beacon when it's getting dark or visibility is poor.天色暗或能见度低时，求救灯光会让屏幕和闪光灯一起闪烁，方便救援队找到你。

FAQ常见问题

Good questions你可能想知道

When can I download AlpMate?什么时候能下载？

AlpMate is in beta testing and coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch. Tap "Notify me at launch" and we'll email you when it's available.雪伴正在测试，即将在 App Store 上架（iPhone 和 Apple Watch）。点「上线时通知我」，上线时我们会发邮件告诉你。

Can I use AlpMate outside New Zealand?在新西兰以外能用吗？

Ski tracking and the community work anywhere — post from any mountain in the world. Piste maps, resort conditions and ski-patrol alerts are for New Zealand ski areas.雪迹记录和雪友社区在哪里都能用，可以在全球任何一座雪山发笔记。雪道地图、雪况和通知救援队目前只支持新西兰的雪场。

Who can see what I post in the community?我在社区发的内容谁能看到？

Posts, comments and your community profile are visible to everyone using AlpMate. Private messages are only between you and the other person. You can delete your posts at any time and choose whether your medals and season stats appear on your profile.笔记、评论和社区主页所有用雪伴的人都能看到；私信只有你和对方能看到。你随时可以删除自己的笔记，也可以选择要不要在主页展示勋章和雪季数据。

Who can join the community?谁可以加入雪友社区？

Anyone can browse. To post, comment, follow or message, you need to sign in, be 16 or older and agree to the community rules. You can report or block anyone in one tap.谁都能浏览。要发笔记、评论、关注或私信，需要登录、年满 16 岁并同意社区规则。看到不合适的人或内容，可以一键举报或拉黑。

Which resorts can I alert ski patrol at?哪些雪场可以通知救援队？

Only resorts connected to AlpMate Patrol. None is connected yet — we're working with resorts on pilots. Everywhere else, the SOS screen still shows your location and accuracy, the resort's patrol number and one-tap 111.只有接入雪伴救援的雪场。目前还没有雪场正式接入，我们正在和雪场洽谈试点。在其他雪场，求助页面仍然会显示你的位置和误差、雪场救援电话，并能一键拨打 111。

Does AlpMate replace calling 111?雪伴能代替拨打 111 吗？

No. AlpMate is a safety support tool, not an emergency service. In a life-threatening emergency, call 111 first — it's one tap away in the app.不能。雪伴是安全辅助工具，不是紧急服务。危及生命时请先拨打 111，App 里一键就能拨。

What if there's no mobile signal?没有信号怎么办？

An alert needs a data connection to reach patrol. Without signal, AlpMate puts calling and texting first, keeps your coordinates on screen to read out, and resends the alert when signal returns. You can also use the rescue light, or your iPhone's satellite SOS where it's available. Tell lift staff if you can — they can radio patrol.求助要有网络才能发到救援队。没有信号时，雪伴会把打电话、发短信排在最前，坐标一直显示在屏幕上可以念给别人听，有信号时自动重发求助；也可以打开求救灯光，或者在支持的地方用 iPhone 的卫星紧急求救。能的话告诉缆车工作人员，他们可以用对讲机呼叫巡逻队。

Is my location shared all the time?我的位置会一直被共享吗？

No. Your location goes to your team only when you join one, and to ski patrol only when you send an alert. Team locations delete after 24 hours. Your community profile shows only your country.不会。只有你加入小队时，位置才会给队友看；只有你发出求助时，才会发给救援队。小队位置 24 小时后自动删除。社区主页上只显示你所在的国家。

Is it free?要钱吗？

Yes, AlpMate is free for skiers and snowboarders. There are no ads and we don't sell data.免费。没有广告，也不卖数据。
